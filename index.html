Chioma Abalogu - Story Teller

About Me

I am a London based Nigerian artist whose work is deeply rooted in the beauty, resilience, and richness of African culture and heritage. My journey into painting began as a personal escape, a hobby that brought me joy and solace. But in 2020, when I was laid off during the COVID-19 pandemic, art became my full-time passion, transforming my life in ways I never anticipated.

Five years in, I have exhibited my work extensively across the UK, sharing visual narratives that celebrate identity, tradition, and the stories of my people. My paintings, often created with oil on linen, blend portraiture and abstraction, capturing the spirit of African heritage with expressive textures and bold colours. I use an underpainting technique with white oil to bring depth and light to my compositions.

Beyond exhibitions, I am committed to using art as a tool for cultural preservation and dialogue. Through my work, I hope to inspire conversations, bridge histories, and connect people to the richness of African traditions. I continue to refine my craft, drawing inspiration from workshops, exhibitions, and the encouragement of fellow artists like Oliver Enweonwu, whose support marked a turning point in my artistic journey.

This is more than just art for me, it is a mission to honour the past, celebrate the present, and shape the future through creativity.

Ogechi(2025)

Oil on canvas · 50 x 70 cm

Inspired by the song Ogechi by Davido, reflects the essence of patience, faith, and divine timing. The women stand with grace and purpose, their adorned figures symbolizing tradition, resilience, and the quiet strength of waiting for the right moment. The deep, earthy tones ground them in history, while the intricate beads, fabrics, and balanced pots tell a story of heritage and endurance. Every brushstroke was guided by the rhythm and message of the song, a reminder that everything unfolds as it should, in its own perfect time. Ogechi is not just a name; it’s a testament to trust, perseverance, and the beauty of destiny.
